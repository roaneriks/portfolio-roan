I make the thing, then shape the strategy behind it.

I'm Roan — a designer who started out in workshops and CAD files and ended up in strategy decks. I trained as an industrial product designer, learning to make things that hold together in the real world, then moved into business and strategic design to shape the thinking that decides what's worth making in the first place.

The path runs from Groningen to Barcelona. I studied Industrial Product Design at Hanze University in the north of the Netherlands, then crossed the continent for a Master in Business Design at Elisava. Two disciplines, one habit of mind: get specific about the problem, then design the whole system around it.

What drives me is the seam between craft and strategy — being able to prototype an idea and pitch the business case for it in the same afternoon. I like work that is useful, honest, and a little bit curious.
