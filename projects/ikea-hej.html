01

IKEA Hej

Future of loyalty & engagement

Role
Business Designer
Context
Elisava × IKEA Xplore Studio
Year
2025–2026
Type
Strategy, Service Design

IKEA Hej is a social mode for the IKEA app that connects Gen Z shoppers by mood and shared interests — turning a solo errand into a moment of belonging. It answers two problems at once: a deepening loneliness crisis, and IKEA's Gen Z blind spot, where 85% of young shoppers never open the app.

01 — Research

A Wall of Evidence

We started on the street. Dozens of short interviews with Gen Z shoppers fed a growing Wall of Evidence — quotes, behaviours, and tensions mapped until the patterns surfaced on their own.

The signal underneath the noise: young people want connection, but the spaces they move through rarely make room for it.

02 — Synthesis

Five core trends

The research distilled into five forces shaping how Gen Z relates to brands, spaces, and each other — the strategic foundation the concept had to stand on.

03 — Concept

The IKEA Hej concept

Hej is a social layer inside the existing app. Opt in, set a mood, and you're matched with nearby shoppers who share an interest — a low-stakes nudge from browsing alone to discovering together.

It rides infrastructure IKEA already has, and gives Gen Z a reason to open the app that has nothing to do with a shopping list.

04 — Delivery

The Multiverse Game

The concept was presented as a Multiverse Game — an interactive format that let stakeholders step into the future scenario rather than read a deck about it.
