01

IKEA Hej

Future of loyalty & engagement

Role
Business Designer
Context
Elisava × IKEA Xplore Studio
Year
2025–2026
Type
Strategy, Service Design

IKEA Hej is a facilitator that helps people make connections by capitalising on the IKEA store experience. Hej brings people together over the personal topic of ways of living to create a meaningful IKEA journey and perhaps a deeper connection or friendship. 86% of Gen Z surveyed were interested in using it.

01 — Challenge

Redefining loyalty for Gen Z

To redefine loyalty and engagement for IKEA, specifically for Gen Z, by using IKEA to solve the problem of lack of connection, loneliness, and the need for mediated connection.

How can IKEA transform loyalty and engagement for Gen Z, turning the store into a space for real connection, belonging, and cultural lore?

02 — Key insight

Human connection is fading

60% of Gen Z do not feel confident starting a conversation without a prior digital connection or specific reason to engage — so environments are stepping in to care for us.

"It's hard to make good friends at this age; I don't know where to find people to meet." — Gen Z interviewee

03 — Concept

The IKEA Hej concept

Facilitates low-friction socialising in physical spaces, lowering social anxiety, respecting emotional timing, and connecting people through the IKEA app's existing tools.

For IKEA: increased store footfall, a humanised brand through human stories, behavioural data on how people live, and lasting customer loyalty.

04 — Delivery

The Multiverse Game

The concept was presented as a Multiverse Game — an interactive format that let stakeholders step into the future scenario rather than read a deck about it.
