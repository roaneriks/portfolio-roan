02

Trends Report

Strategic foresight for retail

Role
Business Designer
Context
Elisava × IKEA Xplore Studio
Year
2025–2026
Type
Research, Foresight

A strategic trend report on the future of loyalty and engagement in retail. An environmental scan fed a Wall of Evidence; Futures Wheels pushed each signal forward in time; and the result was distilled into five core trends, packaged as a creative booklet built to be read, not filed away.

01 — Scan

Environmental scan

A wide sweep across culture, technology, and consumer behaviour — gathering weak signals before they became obvious, and giving the report a horizon wider than next quarter.

02 — Method

Wall of Evidence & Futures Wheels

Signals were mapped onto a Wall of Evidence, then run through Futures Wheels to trace their second- and third-order consequences — turning scattered observations into structured foresight.

03 — Trends

Five core trends

HCD Faces MCD · Create to Survive · Abstinent Self · Mood-Adaptive Living · Synthetic Confidence.

Each trend names a tension retail will have to design around — and a direction it could move in response.

04 — Artefact

A creative booklet

The findings were packaged as a printed booklet — editorial, tactile, and built to circulate, so the strategy lived as an object people picked up rather than a slide they skipped.
