01 — Challenge

How do you make 33 graduating designers, across two masters' programmes, discoverable and memorable to the recruiters, faculty and family who show up for one evening?

02 — Solution

The Menu turns the Grad Show into a restaurant. Students are the chefs, their teams are the tables, and the work they made is the dishes. Visitors — the critics — browse by chef or by table, tap straight from any bio to that team's project, and bookmark favourites onto a personal plate. A stylised floor map gets them to the right seat. Mobile-first and editorial — warm ink, cream paper, terracotta accents, paper-grain texture — so it reads like a menu, not a database.

03 — Built & shipped

Every other project on this site is a concept. GradShow isn't — I designed the direction, then built and shipped the real thing myself with Claude Code: React, Vite, 33 chef profiles and 8 table pages, live at the show and still running today.
