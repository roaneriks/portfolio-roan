Convi-light

Industrial style pendant lamp in conference room.

Role
Product Designer
Context
Internship — Convident digital agency
Year
2021
Type
Product Design, Craft

During my internship, I single-handedly designed and created this industrial, robust looking, pending lamp with plants falling from the sides. The convi-light is located in Convident's conference room.

01 — In the room

The deep green plants contrast with the wood, creating a natural ambiance in the room. The metal frame provides the industrial look.

02 — Construction

Important for the construction is the fact that it must be level. It was also required that the construction is invisible for the eye, by request from the client. In the construction, the heaviest elements are held together by only four bolts. These bolts are connected with chains to a center chain, making it easily possible to correct the spirit level. This construction also made it possible to create the wooden components from just one plank. Driving down the cost.
