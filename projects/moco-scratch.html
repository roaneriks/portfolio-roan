05

Moco Scratch

Future of museum experience

Role
Business Designer
Context
Elisava × Moco Museum Barcelona
Year
2025–2026
Type
Strategy, Experience Design

Moco Scratch is a multi-layered interactive experience that provides visitors with information and connection to the art and artists while delivering an engaging and fun journey. Visitors take a physical scratch card through the museum and at the end receive additional insights and shareable assets digitally.

01 — Challenge

Making art accessible to everyone

Improving the in-person experience of the museum by providing more context and insight on the art in a fun, engaging and immersive way, while keeping in mind that the main purpose of Moco is to make art accessible to everyone.

How might we improve the in-person experience through the different touchpoints within the museum to uncover its full potential?

02 — Key insight

Context unlocks the art

84% of first-time museum visitors with little to no contemporary art experience want more explanation or context about the art.

"Modern and contemporary art is really hard to get without context, story, and meaning giving." — SFMOMA Curators Circle associate

03 — Concept

Phygital scratch card

For visitors: an engaging, interactive experience that deepens and clarifies the art, creates a shareable moment, and helps visualise the full scope of the museum.

For Moco: a phygital layer that makes the visit memorable and immersive, boosts satisfaction and return rates, captures artwork preference insights, builds a CRM through optional email collection, and turns visitors into promoters through personalised shareable Instagram Stories.
