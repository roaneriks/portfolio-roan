02

Moco Scratch

Future of museum experience

Role
Business Designer
Context
Elisava × Moco Museum Barcelona
Year
2025–2026
Type
Strategy, Experience Design

01 — Challenge

How might we improve the in-person experience through the different touchpoints within the museum to uncover its full potential — while keeping art accessible to everyone?

02 — Solution

Moco Scratch is a multi-layered interactive experience that provides visitors with information and connection to the art and artists while delivering an engaging, fun journey. Visitors take a physical scratch card through the museum and at the end receive additional insights and shareable assets digitally. For Moco, it is a phygital layer that boosts satisfaction and return rates, captures artwork-preference insights, and turns visitors into promoters.
