At Dutch Climate Systems I ran installations end to end — surveying sites, producing technical drawings, ordering supply, and keeping stakeholders aligned from first visit to sign-off. Alongside delivery I worked on R&D and communication materials for the company's flagship product, the ICECUBE.

01 — Delivery

End-to-end installations

Surveying, technical drawings, supply ordering, and stakeholder communication — owning the thread that runs from a site visit to a working, signed-off installation.

02 — Product

ICECUBE

The flagship product: dew-point cooling that uses 80% less energy and no Freon. A genuinely different approach to climate control — and the centre of gravity for the R&D and communication work.

03 — R&D

Research & communication

Beyond delivery, I contributed to product R&D and built communication materials that translated a technical system into something a non-engineer could understand and buy into.

04 — School project

ICECUBE for the bedroom

A self-directed project adapting the ICECUBE for residential bedroom use — rethinking scale, noise, and form to bring industrial cooling into a domestic, human-sized context.
