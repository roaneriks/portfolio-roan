01 — School project

In 2022, I collaborated with DCS on a school project to adapt their ICECUBE cooling system for residential use. The goal was to create a sustainable, consumer-friendly solution tailored for the home market. The resulting design focused on bedroom use, prioritizing comfort, quiet operation, and a soft, unobtrusive aesthetic that blends into living spaces.

After graduating, I continued to work on this project. Refining its usability and functionality to further align with DCS's mission of "Cooling the Planet."

02 — Project management

During my job, I dedicated my time to overseeing and executing installations from start to finish. I was responsible for managing the entire process, ensuring every detail was accounted for and that the project runs smoothly. My role involved a variety of tasks, including surveying of new locations, creating technical drawings, ordering supplies, communicating with team members and stakeholders, and handling preparations. Once everything is in place, I oversaw and actively worked on the installation itself.

03 — Product innovation

Besides managing the installation I was also involved in the R&D department, where I collaborate with the team to design and develop future concepts. I was also involved in the creation of communication materials such as brochures, presentations, and social media posts.
