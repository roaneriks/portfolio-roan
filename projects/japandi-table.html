04

Japandi Table

Self-assembly furniture design

Role
Product Designer
Context
MI Studio, Amsterdam
Year
2023
Type
Product Design, Craft

A screwless self-assembly table set built around a single idea: a cross lap joint. Seven planks, no screws and no glue — held together by the geometry of the joinery alone. The Japandi language of light oak, rounded forms, and quiet balance carries the rest.

01 — Principle

The cross lap joint

One joint does all the work. Two planks notch into each other so the structure locks under its own load — the more weight it carries, the more stable it becomes.

02 — System

Seven planks, no screws

The whole set resolves to seven planks. No fasteners, no glue, nothing to strip or loosen — assembly is a matter of seating each piece in the right order.

03 — Language

A Japandi aesthetic

Light oak, softened edges, and honest proportions. The form leans on the Japandi blend of Japanese restraint and Scandinavian warmth — harmony and balance over ornament.

04 — Handover

A user manual

The set ships with a user manual that treats assembly as part of the product — a small, considered ritual rather than a chore to get through.
