Kicktip

A World Cup prediction game for five friends and a €50 pot

Role
Designer & vibe-coder
Context
The group chat
Year
2026
Type
Web app, Side project

01 — Why

Five friends, one group chat, and a growing pile of World Cup score predictions that nobody was keeping track of.

When the knockout rounds started, our WhatsApp group turned into a prediction contest — €10 each, winner takes the pot. The predictions were scattered across the chat and the scorekeeping was on the honour system. So over one weekend I vibe-coded Kicktip: a small web app that remembers every pick, scores it automatically, and settles the arguments for us.

02 — How it works

Scores and fixtures sync live from FIFA's public data, so results and new knockout games appear on their own. Exact score earns 4 points, right goal difference 3, right winner 2 — and predictions lock at kickoff, so no quiet edits at half-time.

Built with Next.js and Claude Code in a weekend, styled like a stadium scoreboard rather than a spreadsheet. The real feature isn't technical: it turned a messy chat thread into a running joke with a leaderboard.
