Nightdrift

A wind-down app we built for a friend, to end the day with rain instead of a feed

Role
Designer & vibe-coder
Context
A friend’s idea, built together
Year
2026
Type
Mobile web app, Side project

01 — Why

The last thing most of us see at night is a feed. A friend of mine wanted hers to be rain.

This one started as someone else’s idea. A friend kept ending her days the way most of us do, in bed and still scrolling, and she wanted something on her phone that would actually help her stop. So we built Nightdrift together, for her. It’s a small React app that asks how you feel (stressed, anxious, just tired, or already calm) and answers with twenty minutes of recorded rain, storms, forest or waves on a slowly spinning vinyl. There is no account to make and no streak to keep up. It plays until you drift off, and that’s the whole product.

02 — How it works

Each mood picks a sound for you. Stressed and anxious get the heavier tracks, tired and calm get the soft ones, and you can always swap mid-session. Every session is twenty minutes. That turned out to be plenty of time to fall asleep, and it means the phone stops on its own instead of waiting for you to stop it. The sounds are real recordings that loop, not generated noise.

The whole app is one HTML file with React inside, vibe-coded with Claude Code. We stripped features after watching how it actually got used at bedtime, which is a humbling kind of user test. The screen we spent the most thought on is the last one. When the timer runs out, the app has nothing left for you to tap. It dims itself and says goodnight.
